Gaming Is Mainstream

Video games are now a core part of Australian culture. Players span across all ages and demographics, with gaming becoming a daily activity for millions.

Games Create Social Connection

Online multiplayer games help Australians connect with friends, families and communities. Many players describe games as an important social activity rather than an isolated hobby.

Mental Health and Wellbeing

Many Australians use games to relax, reduce stress and improve wellbeing. Games increasingly play a role in emotional regulation and digital social support.

Family Experience

For many Australian families, gaming has become a shared activity that supports learning, creativity and family connection.

Australia’s Connected Players

Gaming participation is closely connected to digital access, internet infrastructure and digital inclusion across Australia.

Gaming & The Future Digital Society

Gaming increasingly overlaps with emerging digital lifestyles, AI usage and online participation in Australia.

Conclusion

Gaming in Australia has evolved far beyond a niche pastime for a small group of enthusiasts. It is now a central pillar of modern Australian culture, shaping entertainment, social connections, wellbeing, and digital participation.

Across generations and communities, games offer meaningful opportunities for communication, relaxation, learning, and shared experiences. The data further reveals that gaming is increasingly intertwined with broader digital lifestyles—encompassing online interaction, emotional wellbeing, and emerging technologies.

As Australia continues to grow as a connected digital society, gaming is poised to remain a key cultural and social force, influencing how people interact, engage, and build communities in the years to come.
