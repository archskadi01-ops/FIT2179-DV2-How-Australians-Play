Gaming Is Mainstream

Video games are now a core part of Australian culture. Players span across all ages and demographics, with gaming becoming a daily activity for millions.

Games Create Social Connection

Online multiplayer games help Australians connect with friends, families and communities. Many players describe games as an important social activity rather than an isolated hobby.

Mental Health and Wellbeing

Many Australians use games to relax, reduce stress and improve wellbeing. Games increasingly play a role in emotional regulation and digital social support.
