Gaming Is Mainstream

Video games are now a core part of Australian culture. Players span across all ages and demographics, with gaming becoming a daily activity for millions.

Australia’s Connected Players

Gaming & The Future Digital Society

Gaming increasingly overlaps with emerging digital lifestyles, AI usage and online participation in Australia.

Conclusion
